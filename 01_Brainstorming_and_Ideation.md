ComicCraft – AI Comic Story Creator using Gemini Models

Phase 1 – Brainstorming and Ideation

1. Problem Statement

Creating an interesting comic story manually requires creativity,
story-writing skills, character development, and time. Beginners and
students may find it difficult to create a complete comic story with
characters, scenes, and dialogues.

2. Proposed Solution

ComicCraft is an AI-powered application that uses Google Gemini models
to generate creative comic stories based on user-provided inputs.

3. Project Objectives

• Generate creative comic stories using AI.
• Create characters and dialogues.
• Generate panel-by-panel comic scenes.
• Provide suitable visual prompts for comic panels.
• Make comic story creation simple and user-friendly.

4. Target Users

• Students
• Children
• Story writers
• Comic creators
• Creative-content users

5. Main Features

• Story topic input
• Genre selection
• Character generation
• AI story generation
• Panel-by-panel scene generation
• Dialogue generation
• Image prompts for comic panels

6. Basic Workflow

User Input
↓
ComicCraft Application
↓
Gemini AI
↓
Story and Character Generation
↓
Comic Panel Generation
↓
Dialogue and Image Prompts
↓
Final Comic Story
